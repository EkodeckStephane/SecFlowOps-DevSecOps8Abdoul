\section{Experimental Design}

Table~\ref{tab:campaigns} summarizes the campaigns. The controlled campaign evaluates six configurations: build only, non-blocking scanning, automatic scanning, policy only, agents only, and the combined SecFlowOps configuration. Five generated repositories inject SAST, SCA, secret, Docker, and Kubernetes findings with explicit ground-truth files. Each controlled repository is evaluated three times, yielding 90 runs.

The full protocol campaign materializes the preregistered local design: three repository families, eight scenarios, six configurations, and three repetitions, yielding 432 runs. The eight scenarios are clean, vulnerable dependency, fake secret, reflected XSS, SQL injection, Docker misconfiguration, Kubernetes misconfiguration, and multi-layer. This campaign was completed before ZAP was enabled in the runner; ZAP is therefore evaluated in a separate targeted DAST probe.

The external campaign evaluates five configurations, non-blocking scanning, automatic scanning, policy only, agents only, and the combined SecFlowOps configuration, on five open-source repositories: Requests, Flask, Express, OWASP NodeGoat, and DVNA. Each external repository is evaluated three times, yielding 75 runs. The external campaign uses \texttt{--build-mode skip} to avoid confounding security-pipeline behavior with project-specific dependency installation and test setup.

Two focused campaigns address external validity gaps. A NodeGoat npm study repeats all five configurations three times on OWASP NodeGoat and enables \texttt{npm audit fix --package-lock-only --omit=dev} in the remediation workspace. A separate npm-audit-only baseline repeats the same package-manager remediation three times without SecFlowOps secret remediation or OPA gating. An injected external recall study copies Requests, Flask, and Express, injects six controlled findings under \texttt{secflowops\_injected/}, and evaluates three configurations over three repetitions, yielding 27 runs with complete injected ground truth.
