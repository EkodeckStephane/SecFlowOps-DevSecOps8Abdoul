\begin{abstract}
This manuscript reports SecFlowOps, an executable artifact for evaluating DevSecOps pipeline configurations under scanner, remediation, and policy-gate workloads. The artifact executes Trivy, Semgrep, Gitleaks, npm audit, OWASP ZAP, and OPA; normalizes findings; applies deterministic and npm-based remediations; rescans patched workspaces; and records security and performance evidence. The evaluation comprises a 432-run full controlled protocol, a 90-run controlled size study, a 75-run open-source campaign with static adjudication, a NodeGoat npm-remediation study, an injected external recall study, and a targeted ZAP DAST probe. SecFlowOps reaches recall 1.0 on injected external ground truth and removes all injected target findings after remediation. On NodeGoat, npm-only remediation reduces findings from 78 to 32, while SecFlowOps reduces them to 31 by also removing the repository secret; residual critical findings remain correctly blocked by policy. The results support SecFlowOps as a reproducible evaluation artifact, not as a general proof that arbitrary projects can be automatically repaired.
\end{abstract}
